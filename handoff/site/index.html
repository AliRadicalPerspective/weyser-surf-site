Stuff people ask.

I've never surfed. Is that okay?

Most of Weyser's students start right here. You'll begin in the gentle whitewater, and most people are standing up by the end of their first session. What to expect in your first lesson.

Can kids come?

Yes, and they love it! Kids from age 7 can surf alongside a parent, or with Weyser while you cheer from the beach. More on surf lessons for kids.

Is it safe for kids and beginners?

You're in good hands. Weyser is an ISA-certified surf coach and a certified lifeguard, trained in CPR, and he picks the beach that suits your level each day. Beginners stay where they can stand, on big, soft boards. How lessons work for kids.

We're a family of four or more. Can we all surf?

Yes. Groups are three max so everyone gets real attention. He'll just split you into two sessions back to back.

What if it rains or the waves are bad?

A little rain is no problem, you're getting wet anyway! Lightning is different: if there's lightning, Weyser always reschedules, no questions asked. Same if the waves aren't safe or fun. He'll move you to the next good day, free.

How long is a session?

About 90 minutes in the water, plus a quick briefing on the beach first. Plenty of time to catch a lot of waves.

What time do we surf?

Whenever the ocean is at its best. It depends on the tide and the wind, usually early morning, and Weyser sends you the exact time the day before.

Is there pickup?

Yes. He'll pick you up anywhere in town for $5 per person. Want to try a beach further away? Just ask.

Where do we surf?

Close by. Beginners learn at Playa Hermosa or Playa Carmen. Playa Santa Teresa, the main beach, works for every level depending on the tide and time of day, and Mar Azul is the intermediate beach. All are a short ride from town, and you'll pick the right one together. Feeling adventurous? Cedros or Cabuya make a great trip. Compare the beginner beaches.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days ahead is a good idea in high season (December to April). Last minute works too when there's space, so just ask about tomorrow's waves.

What if the waves are too big?

He'll tell you straight, then find a spot where the waves work for you.

What if I need to cancel?

No stress. Cancelling is free up to 3 hours before your session.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

Not at all, you just need to be comfortable in the water. Beginners stay where they can stand.

How many sessions until I get better?

You'll feel the difference after one. Three is where it really clicks, which is exactly why the Mini Surf Camp exists.

What do I bring?

Just swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, some water and a good mood. Weyser brings everything else.

Can I rent a board after the lesson?

Yes, $15 a day, so you can keep practicing on your own.

How do I pay?

PayPal or cash, on the day. For a Mini Surf Camp, you pay on your first day.

Are lessons in Spanish too?

¡Claro que sí! Lessons are in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer.
