Is surfing safe for kids?

Weyser is an ISA-certified surf coach, lifeguard and CPR certified, and picks the beach for your level each day. With three people max, he's right beside your child the whole time. Beginners stay where they can stand, on soft-top boards.

What if my child is nervous?

Every lesson starts on the sand: how the waves work, the pop-up, what to do when you fall. Nobody goes in until they're ready, and most kids are standing up by the end of the first session.

Can parents surf too?

Yes. Up to three of you in the water together, parents and kids side by side. Or you can watch from the beach and take photos.

We're a family of four or more. Can we all surf?

Yes. Groups are three max so everyone gets real attention. He'll just split you into two sessions back to back.

What should we bring?

Comfortable swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen and some water. Board, rashguard and wax are included.
