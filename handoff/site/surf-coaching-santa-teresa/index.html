Surf coaching in Santa Teresa for improvers.

Weyser coaches surfers who can already stand up but feel stuck: wiping out in the whitewater, missing the good waves, or paddling for waves that never take them. Where most surfers get stuck, and what we do about it

  • Eating whitewater all morning. It’s usually not your arms. It’s the route you take and your timing. I show you how to read the sets from the beach, where the channel is and when to go, so you get out the back with energy left to surf.
  • Watching every good wave go past you. That’s almost always position. We work on where to sit for each peak, how to spot a wave early and when to turn and paddle, so you’re in the right place when it arrives.
  • Paddling like crazy for waves that never quite take you. This is about the last few strokes: where your weight is on the board, how hard you paddle as the wave lifts you, and the angle you take off at. Small changes, big difference.

Three stages. One coach.

01

Get up and go.

Pop up, stance and balance in the whitewater, then turning your board to ride along the wave instead of straight to the beach.

Next stage when you stand up on most waves and can steer a little.

02

Catch your own waves.

Paddling out, reading the sets, positioning and taking off on green, unbroken waves by yourself. We also work on trimming left and right along the face.

Next stage when you catch green waves on your own and ride along them.

03

Level up.

Bottom turns, top turns, making speed and surfing bigger days with confidence. We talk boards too: what to ride as you progress.

From here it’s about mileage, with someone watching every wave.

Every stage comes with the ocean lessons most people skip: currents, etiquette, and where not to be.

How a coaching session works

  1. Before

    Tell me what you’re working on

    Send me a WhatsApp with your level and what feels hard. I check the tide and swell and pick the spot that suits you that day.

  2. On the beach

    We read the ocean together

    Where the peak is, where the current runs, where to paddle out and where not to be. Then one or two goals for the session.

  3. In the water, about 90 minutes

    Feedback on every wave

    With three people max, I see every wave you catch or miss, and tell you what to change on the next one.

  4. After

    What to practise next

    You leave with the one or two things to work on. Video analysis ($30) shows you exactly what I saw; photos of your session are an additional fee.

Why three sessions work

In the first session we find the one thing holding you back and start fixing it. In the second, it starts to become a habit. In the third, you put it together on different waves, and that’s when it clicks. That’s why I built the Mini Surf Camp: three sessions planned as one, $200 just for you, or $170 per person for 2+.

What level do I need to be?

If you can stand up on whitewater waves, you’re ready. Not sure? Pick “Not sure” when you book and I’ll place you.

How many sessions until I get better?

You’ll feel the difference after one. Three is where it really clicks, which is exactly why the Mini Surf Camp exists.

Where do improvers surf?

It depends on the tide and the swell. Mar Azul is the intermediate beach, and Playa Santa Teresa, the main beach, works for every level depending on the tide and time of day. I pick the spot with you each day.

What if the waves are too big?

I’ll tell you straight, then find a spot where the waves work for you.

Can I get photos or video of my session?

Yes. Photos of your session are an additional fee, and video analysis is $30, so you can see exactly what to fix.
